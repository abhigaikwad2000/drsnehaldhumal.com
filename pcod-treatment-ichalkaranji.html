Understanding the Condition

What is PCOD / PCOS?

PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) and PCOS (Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome) are among the most common hormonal disorders affecting women of reproductive age — and in Ichalkaranji and the wider Kolhapur district, they are increasingly seen in young women between 15 and 35 years of age.

In PCOD, the ovaries produce partially mature or immature eggs in excess, which over time become cysts. This leads to an imbalance in estrogen, progesterone, LH, and androgens, disrupting the normal menstrual cycle and causing a cascade of symptoms that affect daily life — from irregular periods and stubborn weight gain to acne and emotional health.

"The good news: PCOD responds exceptionally well to homeopathic treatment when the underlying hormonal pattern and individual constitution are addressed together — no hormonal pills, no surgery."

Unlike allopathic treatment that often relies on oral contraceptive pills to regulate cycles (masking the problem), homeopathy works by gently stimulating the body's own regulatory mechanisms. Over a course of treatment, patients experience gradually normalising cycles, reduced cyst size on ultrasound, clearer skin, and improved energy — naturally.
