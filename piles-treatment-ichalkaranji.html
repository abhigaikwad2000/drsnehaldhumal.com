Understanding the Condition

What are Piles?

Piles, also called hemorrhoids, are swollen veins in or around the rectum and anus. They are very common in adults in Ichalkaranji and the wider Kolhapur district, especially in people with chronic constipation, prolonged sitting, strain during stool, low-fibre diet, or pregnancy-related pressure.

Depending on the type and severity, piles can cause bleeding during passing stool, pain, burning, itching, swelling, or a sensation that the bowel has not cleared fully. Some cases remain mild for a long time, while others gradually become more troublesome because of repeated constipation and straining.

"Piles treatment works best when bowel habits, constipation, diet, strain during stool, and the full symptom pattern are addressed together with regular follow-up."

Some patients seek homeopathic care after recurring bleeding or discomfort despite creams and laxatives, while others want an early non-surgical option. The focus here is on a personalised plan, regular follow-up, and support for symptoms such as pain, bleeding, swelling, and constipation.

Dr. Snehal Dhumal designs an individualised treatment plan for each patient after a thorough case-taking rather than following a generic protocol.
